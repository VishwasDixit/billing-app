Privacy Policy

Billing – Shop POS · Last updated 24 October 2026

Billing is a billing, ordering, kitchen and staff app for restaurants and shops. This page explains what it stores and why. The same policy covers the website and the Android app.

What we store

  • Shop owner account: name, email, mobile number and a password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Shop data you enter: menu, prices, bills, expenses, stock, recipes, tables and settings.
  • Customers of the shop: name and mobile number, when the shop adds them to a bill or a customer orders from a table QR code, plus their udhar (credit) balance with that shop.
  • Staff: name, role, wage, attendance, advances and salary payments, entered by the owner. Staff PINs are stored only as a secure hash.
  • Payment notes: for table orders paid by UPI, the UPI reference number the customer types. The payment itself happens in the customer's own UPI app; we never see bank or card details.

Why we use it

Only to run the app for the shop: making and printing bills, sales reports, kitchen stock, staff salary, and letting the shop contact a customer about their order. We do not sell data, show ads, or use advertising or tracking tools.

Who can see it

  • Each shop's data is visible only to that shop's owner and the staff the owner allows. Cooks see only kitchen screens.
  • Service providers that host or deliver the app: Vercel (hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database), Google (Gmail, to send sign-in codes and reminder emails) and Razorpay (the shop's subscription payment). They process data only to provide their service.

Bluetooth and device permissions

The app uses Bluetooth only when you choose to connect a receipt printer. Bills are kept on the device for a short time so billing keeps working offline, then they are synced.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords and PINs are hashed, logins are rate-limited, and each shop's data is kept separate.

Keeping and deleting data

Shop data is kept while the account is active. Deleted bills stay in the bin for 30 days and are then removed for good. A shop owner can ask for the whole shop and its data to be deleted from Help inside the app; customers of a shop can ask that shop to remove their name and number.

Children

The app is meant for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. For questions, write to us from Help inside the app.
